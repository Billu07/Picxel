# Governance

## Roles
- **Project lead** (repository owner): sets direction, owns `project.md`, approves scope changes and releases.
- **Maintainers:** review and merge pull requests and triage issues. The project lead adds maintainers after sustained, high-quality contributions.
- **Contributors:** everyone who opens issues, pull requests, or discussions.

## How decisions are made
1. **Everyday changes:** decided through pull request review by a maintainer.
2. **Changes to scope, architecture, data handling, or targets:** proposed in an issue, then recorded in `decision.md` before `project.md` changes.
3. **Non-negotiables:** the principles in `project.md` §2, including the detector test, aren't put to a vote.

## Sponsors and partners
Sponsors and partners never influence verdicts, models, or rankings. Every relationship is disclosed publicly.
